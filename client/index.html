      How to play

      1. Hide. Walk anywhere inside the cage and turn to aim. Nobody can see where you are.
      2. Lock in. Stand still when you are happy with your spot and aim.
      3. Reveal. Everyone appears at once, and a short countdown begins.
      4. Fire. All the lasers fire together. Anyone a beam touches is out.
      5. The floor breaks. Every few rounds the outer ice cracks and falls, and the cage closes in.
      6. Win. The last one standing wins.
