      How to play

      1. Hide

        Walk anywhere in the ring and turn to aim. Nobody can see where you are.

      2. Lock in

        Stand still when you like your spot and your aim.

      3. Reveal and fire

        Everyone appears at once. Then all lasers fire together. Anyone a beam touches is out.

      4. The floor breaks

        The outer ice, boards or stone falls away, and the ring closes in. Stand near the edge and you fall.

      5. Win

        The last player standing wins the match.
